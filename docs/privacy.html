DrionTune Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Developer: Gwen Balajediong

DrionTune is a utility application developed by Gwen Balajediong. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

DrionTune does not require an account and does not operate a personal analytics or telemetry server. System telemetry (CPU, RAM, installed app list) is read locally on your device via Shizuku and never uploaded to us.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Unity Ads SDK (Free version): Used to display ads and rewarded trials. Unity Ads may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data according to Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Gumroad API (Pro license verification): When you activate a Pro license key, the key is sent to Gumroad to verify validity. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: Used to check for app updates (version.json) and load developer announcements (announcements.json). Standard GitHub server logs apply.
  • Google Forms (Optional Support / Feedback): If you submit a bug report or feedback via our Google Form, your submission is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. DrionTune Pay (Local GCash / Maya / GoTyme Verification)

If you choose to purchase a Pro license via local PH transfer, your payment ticket is processed by pay.driontune.com (Cloudflare Workers + D1). We store only the name/handle you enter, optional email, payment reference/message, and any uploaded receipt screenshot. Uploaded receipt screenshots are automatically deleted from the database once your payment is approved. We also store an anonymous device installId solely to verify and enforce Pro license activations.

4. Local Storage

DrionTune stores your app preferences, backup snapshots of modified system settings, cached announcements, active payment ticket token (pay_thread_token), and Pro activation status locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

5. Contact & Data Requests

For privacy questions or to request deletion of support messages/submissions, contact: official@driontune.com.
